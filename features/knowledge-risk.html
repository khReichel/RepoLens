What it measures

  • Bus Factor KPIs% of files with a bus factor of one, modules with elevated or critical exposure, and sole-owner developers flagged at risk — at a glance.
  • Key Persons & Module BreakdownWho owns how many files, at what average concentration, and across which modules. Critical / Watch / Stable status makes risk conversations concrete.
  • Temporal OwnershipTracks who owned code when it was written — captures historical accountability that current team rosters miss entirely.
  • Silo Ratio TrendMonth-by-month chart of knowledge concentration. A rising trend signals ownership is narrowing — a strategic warning, not just a snapshot.

When to use it

Developer offboarding

A key engineer hands in their notice. Calyntro maps exactly which files and modules they exclusively own — before the notice period ends.

Succession planning

Identify which developers hold unique knowledge before they become a single point of failure — proactively, not reactively.

Team restructuring

Ensure knowledge is distributed across the new team structure — not accidentally concentrated in one team or one person.
